# Institution Fact Verification Register

Status: working template; no candidate fact in this register is approved for publication until an authorised institution owner signs it off.
Owner: Tagore Group website content owner (to be assigned)
Review cadence: before publication and at least once each academic year; sooner after any operational change.

## Purpose

Create one auditable source of truth for every factual claim published across the TPS and TIER websites. This prevents stale admissions information, incorrect contact routing, unsupported facility or outcome claims, and accidental assumptions that different institutions share one campus or policy.

This register is a governance template, not a source of verified facts. Do not fill gaps by inference or copy details from old websites without confirmation.

## Evidence record — required fields

Create one record per claim, not one record per page.

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Claim ID | Stable identifier, e.g. TPS-CONTACT-001 |
| Institution | Exact legal/public institution name |
| Claim | Exact wording proposed for publication |
| Claim category | Identity, address, contact, board/affiliation, classes, admissions, fees, transport, hostel, facilities, safety, staff, results, award, policy, opening hours |
| Evidence | Current official document, authoritative register, signed management confirmation or other primary evidence |
| Evidence location | Controlled document path/reference; do not store passwords or OTPs |
| Evidence date | Date the evidence was issued or checked |
| Approver | Named role/person authorised to approve this claim |
| Approval | Pending / approved / rejected / expired |
| Scope | Which site, page, language and academic year may use the claim |
| Last verified | Date of last verification |
| Review due | Next review date or triggering event |
| Publication status | Draft / published / withdrawn |
| Notes | Limitations, caveats or required wording |

## Approval workflow

1. **Capture:** content editor writes the exact claim and identifies the institution it belongs to.
2. **Source:** attach or reference current primary evidence. A verbal recollection or legacy page is a lead to verify, not sufficient evidence for a material claim.
3. **Review:** the responsible institution head/department owner checks accuracy, scope, date and public wording.
4. **Approve:** record an explicit approval and the permitted pages/languages/academic year.
5. **Publish:** implement only the approved wording; add a review date in the content record.
6. **Monitor:** recheck on the review date and whenever fees, contact ownership, admissions, affiliation, policy or facilities change.
7. **Withdraw:** promptly remove or qualify expired, disputed or unsupported claims; retain the audit record.

## High-risk claim checks

- **Admissions:** current academic year, classes open, eligibility, age criteria, documents and application steps must be approved. Never imply a seat or admission is guaranteed.
- **Fees and concessions:** use a current signed fee circular; state applicable year and inclusions. Do not publish an inferred average fee.
- **Boards and affiliation:** verify the institution, campus, affiliation number, validity and permitted wording against the issuing authority.
- **Facilities and safety:** distinguish a facility that exists from one that is available to a particular class/hostel resident. Do not promise supervision, security, transport coverage or medical support without written policy evidence.
- **Results and achievements:** define cohort, exam, year, denominator and source. No selective or unverifiable ranking claims.
- **Staff profiles:** verify current role, qualification, credential and consent to publish personal profile/media.
- **Contact details:** verify mailbox/phone ownership, who monitors it, staffed hours and correct department routing.
- **Maps and locations:** verify each institution's physical campus, entrance, postal address and pin independently.

## Release gate

A page is not ready for public release if it contains a material factual claim that has no current evidence, named approver, permitted scope or review date. Where a detail is not confirmed, omit it or state clearly that the family should contact the institution for current information.

Do not put student names, marksheets, health details, identity documents, family contact details or other sensitive personal data in this public-content register. Store approval evidence in an access-controlled organisational location and reference it here by ID.
