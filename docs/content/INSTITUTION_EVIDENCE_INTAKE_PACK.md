# Institution Evidence Intake Pack

Status: blank collection template; **no fact below is verified or approved for publication**.
Purpose: collect evidence consistently for each institution without assuming shared campuses, contacts, programmes or policies.
Handling: store supporting documents in an access-controlled organisational drive. Put only a non-sensitive reference/ID here; never commit student records, identity documents, private staff contact details, passwords or OTPs.

## How to use

1. Make a copy of the institution worksheet for each campus/entity and academic year.
2. An authorised staff member fills the proposed public wording and identifies the primary evidence.
3. The institution head or delegated approver confirms accuracy, scope, wording and review date.
4. Website editors publish only approved fields. Leave unknown fields blank; do not infer them from an old website, directory listing or another institution.
5. Reconfirm time-sensitive information before each admissions cycle and whenever ownership/policy changes.

## Central review checklist

- [ ] Every institution has its own worksheet; no details copied across institutions without verification.
- [ ] Legal/public names and campus locations are confirmed.
- [ ] Public contact channels are tested and have accountable owners.
- [ ] Board/affiliation details are confirmed against current authoritative records.
- [ ] Admissions, fees and eligibility refer to the correct academic year.
- [ ] Hostel, transport, facilities and safety wording is supported by current policies.
- [ ] Results, awards and numerical claims include source, cohort and date.
- [ ] English/Hindi wording has been reviewed by a competent reviewer.
- [ ] Approvals and review dates are recorded.
- [ ] No student/family sensitive data or confidential documents are stored in Git.
