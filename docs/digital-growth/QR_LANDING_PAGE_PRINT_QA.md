# QR Landing Pages — GROW-22

Status: first-party lightweight landing pages implemented; **no QR campaign or public distribution is approved**.

## Destinations

- TPS: `https://www.tpsgudhagorji.com/start`
- TIER: `https://www.tieredu.org/start`

Both routes are text-first, require no login, include human contact paths, and are marked noindex so they do not become thin search landing pages. They do not collect data, trigger analytics, or add campaign tracking parameters.

## Print / QR checklist before distribution

1. Obtain written approval for the campaign purpose, audience, dates, copy and accountable owner.
2. Generate QR codes offline with a free tool; encode only the approved first-party HTTPS destination above.
3. Keep a readable printed URL beside each QR code.
4. Test scans on current Android and iOS devices, in realistic lighting and at final print size.
5. Test the landing page on a low-bandwidth mobile connection and with JavaScript disabled where practical.
6. Confirm all links, contact details and claims with the institution before printing.
7. Do not encode referrer names, phone numbers, student identifiers, class rosters or household data.
8. Do not distribute until the relevant institution approves the print proof.

## Measurement boundary

The current pages are deliberately measurement-free. Campaign attribution, referral codes, analytics, ERP lead attribution and incentives remain disabled until the separate privacy, owner and ERP approvals are complete.
