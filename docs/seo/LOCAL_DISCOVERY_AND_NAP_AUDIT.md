Do not publish a location, phone number, opening hour, category, affiliation or service claim until the responsible institution confirms it.

## Institution-level NAP source of truth

Create one reviewed record per public-facing institution with:

- Canonical public name and any approved alternate name.
- Physical address, entrance location, postal code and visitor directions.
- Primary phone and email, department ownership and staffed contact hours.
- Official website URL and institution-specific contact page.
- Public opening hours, holiday exceptions and appointment requirements.
- Institution category/board/programme facts with authoritative evidence source.
- Record owner, last verified date, next review date and approval status.

Use a single controlled source for site footer, contact page, structured data, maps/directions links and authorised business profiles. Do not copy group-wide contact details to every institution without confirmation.

## Local search implementation backlog

1. **NAP verification:** obtain signed-off details from each institution head/office owner; record evidence and review date.
2. **Location pages:** provide a useful institution-specific page with verified address, directions, entrance guidance, contact channels and accessibility/visitor notes where confirmed.
3. **Map links:** use a verified map pin and a plain directions link. Add an embedded map only if it improves usability and privacy/performance impact is accepted.
4. **Structured data:** use School, EducationalOrganization or CollegeOrUniversity only where entity type is accurate; use PostalAddress, GeoCoordinates, openingHoursSpecification and telephone only with verified values. Avoid duplicate entities and invented coordinates.
5. **Local content:** answer real visitor questions (where to enter, how to request a visit, whom to contact) rather than creating thin city/neighbourhood doorway pages.
6. **Business profiles:** claim/update only profiles the organisation owns or is authorised to manage; preserve the exact institution name and avoid keyword stuffing.
7. **Search Console:** create separate verified properties for tpsgudhagorji.com and tieredu.org only after owner authorisation and domain access.
8. **Review governance:** respond to reviews professionally; never buy reviews, condition incentives on positive sentiment or expose student/parent personal information.
9. **Monitoring:** review duplicate listings, changed phone/address/hours and broken directions quarterly; route corrections to the record owner.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every public NAP field has a named institutional approver and evidence source.
- Each physical campus has its own verified pin; no shared address is assumed.
- Site footer, contact page, schema and authorised profiles agree.
- Directions work on mobile and lead to the correct visitor entrance.
- Contact links reach a monitored channel with clear staffed hours.
- No tracking or third-party map embed is enabled before privacy/performance review.
- No Search Console, profile, DNS or account change occurs without explicit owner approval.

## Inputs needed before implementation

- Institution-by-institution address, map pin and entrance confirmation.
- Current phone/email recipient and department ownership.
- Public office hours and visitor access process.
- Authorised Google Business Profile and Search Console access via invitation (never passwords or OTPs).
